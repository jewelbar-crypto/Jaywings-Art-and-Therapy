Working With Me

I take a strengths-based, person-centred approach, drawing on humanistic and psychodynamic frameworks. Counselling, narrative therapy, and art therapy techniques are combined as needed to:

  • Support self-understanding and emotional regulation
  • Provide grounding and mindfulness
  • Foster reflection and insight
  • Encourage progress toward your goals

I am not afraid to gently challenge clients when it supports growth, and I am committed to ongoing professional development to ensure my practice remains informed and responsive.

My Background

I hold a Master of Mental Health (Art Therapy) from the University of Queensland Medical School and a Bachelor of Education from QUT, with two decades’ teaching experience. My master’s incorporated extensive practical counselling experience and in-depth study of art therapy. My work as an educator gave me hands-on experience supporting neurodivergent clients and applying trauma-informed approaches.

Creative Work in Sessions

I bring a strong background in visual arts and education, with experience across a wide range of media. This allows sessions to be adapted thoughtfully to each client's needs, comfort, and goals.

Where helpful, sessions may include creative approaches alongside talking therapy. Creative processes may involve drawing, painting, printmaking, sculpture, mixed media, creative writing, or photography, though no prior experience or artistic skill is required.

The focus is on exploration and process rather than outcome. Participation in art-making is always optional, and sessions may also involve reflection, image-based work, or non-verbal exploration without creating physical artwork. Mistakes are viewed as opportunities for learning, and materials are used flexibly rather than prescriptively.

Sessions

Sessions can be offered in person or online. Each session is structured around your needs and pace, whether you are seeking short-term support, ongoing therapy, or a combination of talking and creative approaches.
