JADE JAGO

I’m Jade Jago — Counsellor & Art Therapist (MMH, BEd). I offer confidential counselling and art therapy to support adults with anxiety, low mood, relationship difficulties and life transitions. Sessions are tailored to your needs; creative approaches are optional.

I welcome clients of all genders, sexual identities, and backgrounds; sessions provide a safe, non-judgemental space to explore your experiences at your own pace.

Sessions are currently offered online, with additional options available by arrangement.

What I Offer

  • Individual counselling — Person-centred therapy to explore feelings, build insight, and develop coping strategies.
  • Art therapy — A creative, non-verbal approach to help process emotions, experiences, and self-expression.
  • Short- or long-term support — Flexible interventions to meet your needs, whether you want brief guidance or ongoing therapeutic work.
