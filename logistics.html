Logistics

Information about pricing, rebates, payment and cancellation policies.

Pricing

  • Initial consultation — 60 minutes: $160
  • Standard session — 50 minutes: $140
  • Extended session — 80 minutes: $210

Health Fund Rebates

Some private health insurance extras policies provide rebates for counselling or allied health sessions. Rebates depend on your insurer and level of cover — please check with your fund prior to booking. Receipts/invoices can be provided on request.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Shorter notice may incur a cancellation fee (full session rate) unless exceptional circumstances apply.

Payment & Receipts

Payment is accepted by electronic transfer, card, or cash (in person). Receipts suitable for insurance or rebate claims are available on request.

Session Format

Sessions are offered online and in-person. Please advise at booking which you prefer.
