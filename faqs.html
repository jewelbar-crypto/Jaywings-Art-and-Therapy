FAQs

Frequently asked questions about pricing, rebates, payments and cancellations.

Fees & Policies

Session length & fees

  • 50‑minute session — $200 AUD
  • 75‑minute session — $300 AUD

The first session is a standard session. It gives us time to explore what brings you to therapy and to check fit.

Cancellations & missed sessions

All appointments are a reserved block of time dedicated to you. Please provide at least 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. For online sessions (e.g., via Zoom), arriving more than 15 minutes late without notice will be treated as a missed session and the full session fee will apply.

Payment

Payment is required at the conclusion of each session.

Art created in sessions

Any artwork created during sessions remains your intellectual property. It will not be used by Jade Jago for promotional or commercial purposes without your explicit written consent.

Session types & locations

Sessions are offered online and, where appropriate, in clients’ homes. Creative activities are optional and tailored to your comfort and goals.

Additional information

Full practice policies, including consent and confidentiality, are provided prior to your first session.
